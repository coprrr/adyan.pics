ADYAN.PICS - PHOTO REFERENCE

REGULAR PHOTOS
- Crop ratio: 4:3 landscape.
- Recommended export: 2000 x 1500 pixels.
- Smaller originals: do not enlarge just to reach this size. Crop to 4:3 and keep the available resolution.
- Crop first, then resize with proportions locked. Do not stretch images.

PLACEHOLDER SIZE
- Placeholders have a 4:3 ratio, not a fixed pixel size.
- The gallery adapts to the screen width.
- On a desktop with the gallery at its maximum width, each regular slot is 348 x 261 CSS pixels.
- The desktop layout has three columns, with 18 pixels between columns.
- At screen widths of 700 pixels or less, the gallery switches to one column.
- Exported image pixels do not need to match the slot pixels. Larger exports help keep photos sharp on high-resolution screens and in the enlarged viewer.
- Gallery images currently keep their own proportions. Export at 4:3 for consistent regular photo heights.

WIDE PHOTO - CURRENTLY MISC PHOTO 7
- Spans two columns on desktop, one column on phones.
- Optional crop ratio: 8:3 landscape.
- Recommended export for that crop: 2400 x 900 pixels.
- At maximum desktop gallery width, the wide slot is 714 CSS pixels wide (two 348-pixel columns plus an 18-pixel gap).
- An 8:3 image at that width is about 268 CSS pixels tall. The gap means it is slightly taller than a regular 261-pixel slot.
- The website preserves image proportions; the wide photo does not have to be cropped to 8:3.
